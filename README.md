# EC's Undergraduate Dissertation Research Project in Health Policy

This details the process I took in my undergraduate research project as part of my honours degree in Social and Public Policy at the University of Glasgow. I designed and researched a topical area of health policy, using quantitative and qualitative methods in the form of an online questionnaire and focus group. The title and research questions are as follows:

### Patient-Centred Healthcare in Remote and Rural Scotland: A Quality Evaluation of the 2018 General Medical Services Contract

RQ1: What factors influence patient satisfaction with general practices in remote and rural areas of Scotland?
RQ2: What are the key patient priorities for general practice in remote and rural areas of Scotland?
RQ3: To what extent are patient priorities being upheld in remote nad rural general practices under the aims and outcomes of the 2018 General Medical Services Contract?

### Dissertation Outline ###
The final dissertation included: an abstract; literature review; methodology and methods; research results; findings and discussion; and conclusion. This example project replicates the process I took to design and analyse the **quantitative research data** only. The aim of the online survey was to gather information on the factors which yield high patient satisfaction in remote and rural areas of Grampian, Scotland, in accordance with RQ1.

### Ordianal Logistic Regression Analysis ###
To analyse the quesitonnaire data an ordinal logistic regression model was administered using R software. This type of model allows for comparison between satisfaction levels, while accounting for other factors. The model fit was tested with the McFadden R2 and the Proportional Regression Assumption, which identifies whether the model results can be relied upon. Multicollinearity was tested using multi correspondence analysis, a method of graphic categorical variables to check for similarities. Odds ratios were used to interpret the results of the model.
